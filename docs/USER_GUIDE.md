# Haulbook user guide

Haulbook is built around one idea: **every job is a load, and each load tells you what to do next.** If you are ever unsure, open the Home screen. It lists what needs your attention.

## 1. Getting started

**First visit.** Create your sign-in and enter your company name, currency and whether you are VAT registered. That is all that is needed to start.

**Welcome tour.** A six-step tour explains the flow. You can skip it and replay it any time from **Help & guide**.

**Getting started checklist.** Your Home screen shows a checklist until it is complete:

1. Add your logo and company address
2. Add your banking details (shown on invoices)
3. Add your first customer
4. Add your first subcontractor
5. Book your first load
6. Raise your first invoice
7. Record your first payment

Tap any item to go straight there.

**Install it on your phone.** iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen. It then opens like an app.

## 2. Finding your way around

**On a phone** the bar at the bottom has:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Home | What needs attention today, and your key numbers |
| Loads | All your jobs |
| **+** (blue) | Create anything: load, invoice, quote, bill, expense, customer, subcontractor |
| Invoices | What customers owe you |
| More | Everything else: quotes, bills, expenses, payments, customers, subcontractors, reports, settings, help |

**On a computer** the same sections are in the menu on the left.

Every list has a **search box** and **filter buttons** (for example Unpaid, Overdue, Paid).

## 3. The daily flow

```
1. Book the load         + → New load
2. Truck collects        Open load → Mark in transit
3. Delivered             Open load → Mark delivered → add POD photo
4. Invoice customer      Open load → Create invoice → Save → Send
5. Customer pays         Open invoice → Record payment
6. Sub sends invoice     Open load → Capture bill
7. Pay subcontractor     Open bill → pay from your bank → Record payment
```

Each load shows a progress bar with these steps and a **Next step** box with the right button, so you never need to remember where you are.

## 4. Customers and subcontractors

- **Customers** are the companies you move freight for. You invoice them.
- **Subcontractors** are the trucking companies you give work to. You pay them.

**To add one:** + → Customer (or Subcontractor). Only the name is required. Useful extras:

- **Phone / email** give you one-tap Call, WhatsApp and Email buttons.
- **Address and VAT number** are printed on the customer's invoices.
- **Payment terms (days)** sets the due date automatically on new invoices or bills.
- **Banking details** (subcontractors) are shown on the bill when it is time to pay.

Opening a customer or subcontractor shows what is owed, what is overdue, and their invoices or bills and loads.

**Archive** hides a contact you no longer use without losing history.

## 5. Loads

A load is one job.

**To book a load:** + → New load.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Customer | Who the job is for. "Add a customer" is right under the field. |
| Customer reference / PO | Their order number. Carried onto the invoice. |
| Pickup from / Deliver to | Places, as you would say them. |
| Pickup / delivery date | Delivery date can be left blank until it happens. |
| Subcontractor | Who is carrying it. Leave blank if you use your own truck. |
| Truck, driver, driver phone | Optional. The driver's number becomes a tap-to-call link. |
| You charge the customer | Your rate, before VAT. |
| You pay the subcontractor | Their rate, before VAT. |

Your **margin** is shown as you type.

**Updating a load.** Open it and use the buttons in the Next step box: *Mark in transit*, *Mark delivered*.

**Proof of delivery.** Under Documents, choose "Proof of delivery" and tap **Add photo / file** to take a photo of the signed POD or attach a PDF. This ticks "POD received" for you. You can also attach rate confirmations and the subcontractor's invoice.

**Filters:** Active, To invoice, Delivered, POD missing, Awaiting sub bill, Cancelled.

**Cancelling.** "Cancel load" keeps the record but removes it from to-do lists and reports. It can be reopened.

## 6. Quotes

Use a quote to give a customer a price before the job.

1. + → New quote. Choose the customer, describe the job, enter the price.
2. Save, then **Send** to share the PDF.
3. When they accept, open the quote and tap **Convert to invoice**. A draft invoice with the same lines is created. Or mark it **Accepted** / **Declined** to keep track.

## 7. Invoices

**From a load (fastest).** Open a delivered load → **Create invoice**. The customer, reference, description and rate are filled in. Add more lines if needed (waiting time, tolls) and Save.

**Several loads on one invoice.** + → New invoice → choose the customer → **Add loads**. Tick each load to add it as a line.

**From scratch.** + → New invoice and type the lines yourself.

Each line has a quantity, a price and a VAT tick box. Totals update as you type. The due date is set from the customer's payment terms and can be changed.

**Sending.** A new invoice is a **Draft**. Open it and tap **Send**:

- **Share the PDF**: opens your phone's share menu (WhatsApp, email, etc.) with the PDF attached.
- **WhatsApp message**: sends a text summary with the amount and your banking details.
- **Email**: opens your email app with the message written for you.
- **Download PDF** / **Print**.

Sharing marks the invoice as **Sent**. If you sent it another way, tap **Mark as sent**.

**What is on the invoice.** Your logo, company details, VAT number, the customer's details, the lines, VAT, total, balance due, your banking details with the invoice number as reference, and your notes and terms.

**Statuses**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Not sent yet. Does not count as money owed. |
| Awaiting payment | Sent, not yet due. |
| Part paid | Some money received. |
| Overdue | Past the due date. Shows the number of days. |
| Paid | Fully paid. |
| Void | Cancelled. Kept for the record. |

## 8. Receiving payments

1. Open the invoice.
2. Tap **Record payment**.
3. The balance is filled in. Change the amount if they paid only part.
4. Choose the date and how they paid, add the bank reference if you like, and save.

The invoice updates straight away. All payments are listed under **More → Payments**.

## 9. Subcontractor bills and paying them

A **bill** is the invoice a subcontractor sends you.

**To capture one:** open the load → **Capture bill** (or + → Subcontractor bill). Enter their invoice number, date and amount. Choose "No VAT" if they are not VAT registered. The due date follows their payment terms.

**Knowing when to pay.** The bill shows one of two notices:

- Green: *Your customer has paid for this load. It is safe to pay.*
- Amber: *Your customer has not paid for this load yet*, with a link to the unpaid invoice.

Bills that are safe to pay also appear on Home under **To do** and in **Bills → Ready to pay**.

**To pay:** the bill shows the subcontractor's banking details and the reference to use. Make the payment from your bank, then tap **Record payment** on the bill.

## 10. Expenses

For running costs that are not subcontractor bills: fuel, tolls, repairs, airtime, bank charges.

+ → Expense. Choose a category, enter the total paid, and tick "This includes VAT" if you have a tax invoice (the VAT is worked out for you and can be corrected).

Expenses feed the Profit & loss and VAT reports.

## 11. Reports and statements

Under **More → Reports**:

| Report | Answers |
|---|---|
| Profit & loss | What did I make? Income less subcontractor costs less expenses. |
| Load profitability | Margin per load and per customer. |
| Who owes you | Unpaid invoices per customer, by how late they are. |
| Who you owe | Unpaid bills per subcontractor, by how late they are. |
| VAT report | VAT charged less VAT paid, for your return. |

Pick a period with the buttons (This month, Last month, …) or set your own dates. Every report can be printed.

**Customer statement.** Open a customer → **Statement**. Shows opening balance, invoices, payments and amount due, with your logo and banking details. Print it or save it as a PDF from the print screen.

**Export.** Reports → Export your data downloads spreadsheets (CSV) of invoices, bills, payments, expenses, loads and contacts for your accountant.

## 12. Settings

| Section | What you set |
|---|---|
| Company & logo | Logo, registered and trading name, registration number, contact details, address |
| Banking details | The account printed on invoices and statements |
| Invoice settings | Currency, VAT registration and rate, number prefixes and next numbers, default payment terms, default notes, invoice colour |
| Team | Add staff, set roles, reset passwords, deactivate |
| My account | Your name and password |

**Coming from another system?** Under Invoice settings, set "Next invoice number" so your numbering carries on.

**Roles**

| | Staff | Admin | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loads, quotes, invoices, bills, payments, expenses | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Change settings, manage team | | ✓ | ✓ |
| Void invoices and bills, delete payments, delete records | | ✓ | ✓ |
| Cannot be deactivated | | | ✓ |

## 13. Fixing mistakes

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Wrong amount on an unpaid invoice | Open it → Edit |
| Invoice already has a payment | An admin deletes the payment (bin icon), then Edit |
| Invoice should not exist | Draft: Delete. Sent: an admin taps Void invoice |
| Recorded a payment by mistake | An admin opens the invoice or bill and taps the bin icon next to the payment |
| Captured a bill by mistake | An admin opens it → Delete (or Void to keep a record) |
| Marked a load delivered too early | Open the load → Undo delivered |
| Forgot a password | An owner or admin resets it under Settings → Team |

Sent invoices cannot be deleted, only voided. This keeps your invoice numbers complete, which tax authorities expect.

## 14. Quick answers

**Does VAT get added automatically?** Yes, if "We are VAT registered" is ticked. Untick VAT on any line that is zero-rated.

**Are rates on a load including VAT?** No. Load rates are before VAT. VAT is added on the invoice and the bill.

**Can I part-pay a subcontractor?** Yes. Change the amount when recording the payment.

**Can two people work at the same time?** Yes. Numbers are issued safely so two invoices never get the same number.

**Where is my data?** In your own TiDB database. Nothing is stored on the phone.
